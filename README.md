# Finchglow-Travels-Projects

Project Topic: Delivering an International AGM Travel Program During COVID-19

Organization: Finchglow Travels | Client: MRS Oil

Role: Account Manager / Project Lead

Problem

An international annual general meeting required coordinated travel, accommodation, conference facilities, transportation, and leisure activities. COVID-19 disrupted planning and changed costs, travel conditions, health requirements, and supplier arrangements. The trip's scale also created challenges around the client's credit limit.

Task

I needed to coordinate the client, internal teams, and destination suppliers; revise the plan as conditions changed; and prepare the travel program for successful delivery.

Action

Clarified the destination, traveler requirements, meeting needs, and activities with the client.

Worked with travel consultants on flights and with Finance on credit and payment arrangements.

Coordinated Finchglow Holidays, hotels, transport providers, and conference venues.

Reassessed the budget and schedule following pandemic disruptions.

Strengthened risk management, contingency arrangements, emergency planning, and medical support, including a standby doctor.

Managed communication and readiness across the participating teams and suppliers.

Results

According to my project experience, the trip proceeded successfully in March 2021 after replanning. The case study demonstrates how I coordinated a complex travel program under changing conditions. Kenya, the modeled traveler count, naira budgets, satisfaction scores, and budget-variance figures are illustrative scenario details, not confirmed client records.

Skills demonstrated: Client management, integrated planning, supplier coordination, financial coordination, contingency planning, and risk management.
